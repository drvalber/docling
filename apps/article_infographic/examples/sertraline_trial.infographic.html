Resultados
At week 12, HAM-D scores fell by 9.8 ± 5.2 points with sertraline and 5.7 ± 5.4 points with placebo (mean difference −4.1, 95% CI −5.6 to −2.6; p < 0.001).

Sertraline is superior to placebo for late-life depression treated in primary care and is well tolerated.

Mensagens para levar

  • Sertraline is superior to placebo for late-life depression treated in primary care and is well tolerated.
  • Longer trials should evaluate relapse prevention and outcomes in patients with cognitive impairment.

Implicações clínicas

  • Sertraline produced a clinically meaningful reduction in depressive symptoms compared with placebo in older primary care patients.
  • In clinical practice, the results support sertraline as a first-line option for late-life depression when psychotherapy is unavailable.

Limitações

  • Limitations include the exclusion of patients with dementia, which limits generalizability, and the relatively short 12-week follow-up.
